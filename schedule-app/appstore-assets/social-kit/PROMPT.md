## What Keystone is

One calm app for your calendar, goals, tasks and the people in your life. Local-first (your data lives on your phone, works offline, no ads, no data selling).

- **Home:** leads with what's next, then your day. Several layouts and a daily challenge.
- **Planner:** day, week and month views, drag to reschedule, double-booking and travel-time warnings, day and week templates.
- **Goals:** daily and weekly goals with streaks, a day streak that forgives a missed day, rings for today and the week.
- **People:** a personal contact book with "it's been a while" nudges, follow-ups, a timeline per person, birthdays that show up on their own, status groups.
- **Map:** pins for places and people, clusters, directions, and satellite or hybrid views. Standard, light and dark maps are kept on the phone as you view them, so they work with no signal.
- **Canvas calendar (Pro):** paste your school's Canvas calendar link and assignments and due dates appear on your Keystone calendar, with a colour per class.
- **Home screen widgets (Android):** quick add event/task/note/person, next up, goal logging, streak, week strip, people to follow up, month view.
- **Look:** light and dark, 26 colour themes, 7 Pro backgrounds.
- **Accountability partners:** share goal progress with someone you trust.
