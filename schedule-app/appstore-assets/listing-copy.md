## ASO notes

- **Primary keyword bet:** "calendar" + "goals" together is the actual differentiator — most
  competitors (Sunsama, Akiflow, Amie, Motion) sell scheduling alone; personal-CRM apps and habit
  trackers are separate categories entirely. Ranking for combined intent ("planner with goals",
  "calendar and habit tracker") is more winnable than competing head-on for "calendar app" alone.
- **What NOT to lead with:** avoid "AI" or "smart" as the headline hook — every competitor already
  claims that word, and it's not what the actual feature set differentiates on.
- **Category:** Productivity (primary). Consider Lifestyle as a secondary category on iOS if
  allowed — the contacts/relationships angle fits there too.
- **Next step (Phase 2 of the roadmap):** run this keyword list through App Store Connect's
  search-term data or a tool like AppTweak/App Radar, and replace the guesses above with
  validated terms before Phase 1's listing copy is finalized for submission.
