## Full description (4000 max)

```
Keystone puts your calendar, your goals, and the people in your life in one app, because that is how a real day works.

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
• A Home screen that leads with what is next, then lays out the rest of your day on a simple timeline
• Choose how it looks: Today, a tile dashboard (Pro), Calm, or the classic cards
• A weekly review at the end of each week: what you finished, what slipped, who to call, and one thing to do next week

A CALENDAR THAT STAYS OUT OF YOUR WAY
• Day, week, and month views, with free time shown so you can see where to fit things
• Drag to reschedule in 5 minute steps, or select several events and move them together
• Warnings for double bookings and tight travel times
• Day and week templates for the schedule you repeat

HABITS YOU WILL ACTUALLY KEEP
• Daily and weekly goals with streaks, and a day streak that forgives one missed day a week
• Tasks with subtasks, repeating tasks, and a clear "left today" count with a little celebration when you finish
• Reminders with buttons on the notification: mark a task done, log a goal, or snooze, without opening the app

STAY CLOSE TO THE PEOPLE WHO MATTER
• A simple personal contact book: when did I last talk to Mom?
• Gentle nudges when it has been a while, a favorites row, and an A to Z list that is fast with hundreds of contacts
• Birthdays and anniversaries that appear on their own
• Accountability partners: share goal progress with someone you trust

A MAP OF YOUR PLACES
• Pin the places and people that matter, with search, clusters that stay readable, distance, and one tap directions
• See today's events on the map

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE
• A home screen widget showing your next items, with a quick add button
• Share text, links, or an address from any app straight into Keystone as a task, note, or pin
• Haptics you can dial down or turn off
• 22 color themes, light and dark, and a home screen you can rearrange
• Works offline. Your data lives on your device.

PRO (one time purchase, not a subscription)
Streak and Bento layouts, contact groups, goal history, cloud sync across devices, Google Calendar sync, calendar import and export, and more. Pay once, keep it, including everything added later.

No ads. No selling your data.
```
