**Where do most people drop off?** After they say they're interested: I haven't given them a specific price and a clear ask, so nobody has bought yet.

## Part 2 — Channel and AI plan

**Channel:** Warm outreach (texting people I know), through returned-missionary and ward friends and BYU-Idaho classmates.

**Prompt used:**

> I'm a college student in Rexburg, Idaho, selling Keystone Pro (a one-time upgrade with extra features and customization for a planner/goals/people app built for returned missionaries) to returned missionaries and college students for $9.99. Using warm outreach (texting people I know), give me the 3 exact steps to get more customers in the next 7 days with almost no money, plus one message or post I can copy. No jargon.

**3 steps:**

1. **Go back to the people who already said they're interested (today).** Text each one personally and give them the price and the ask. Right now they're interested but haven't been asked to buy.
2. **Make buying and trying easy (days 2–3).** Offer them a short walkthrough or screenshots, and let them try the free app first. Ask for a yes or no by a set date, such as Friday.
3. **Ask each buyer for two referrals (days 4–7).** Text the friends they name, mention who sent you, and follow up once. Send a quick thank-you to everyone who answers.

**Example message to copy:**

> Hey [Name]! You said you wanted to try Keystone, the planner I built for returned missionaries (goals, people to follow up with, calendar, widgets, all offline, no ads). Pro is a one-time $9.99 with no subscription, and it includes everything I add later. Want me to send you the link to try it? If you like it, you can upgrade this week, and I'm happy to show you around first.
