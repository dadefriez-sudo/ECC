# Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Keystone is made by KeyStone Software ("we", "us"). It is built around one idea: your calendar, your goals and your people are your data, and they stay on your device by default. This page explains what Keystone collects, when anything leaves your device, who else sees it, and how to delete it.

## The short version

- You can use Keystone's free features without an account. In that case nothing you enter is sent to us.
- We do not run ads, we do not use analytics or third-party trackers, and we do not sell your data.
- A few optional features send data off your device. Each one is listed below, with what it sends and why.
- You can delete your data on your phone, and your account and everything stored for it, at any time.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter (events, goals, tasks, notes, contacts, map pins and settings) is stored on your device. Keystone works fully offline, and none of this is sent anywhere unless you turn on one of the optional features below.

If you turn on automatic backups, or export a backup by hand from Settings → Your data, Keystone saves a copy of your data as a file in your device's Documents folder, outside the app's private storage. Backups are locked with a password you choose. That file is visible to other apps on your device that have file access, and it never leaves your device unless you move it yourself.

A few features keep small working copies on your device so they can do their job. None of them send anything on their own:

- **Home screen widget (Android).** The widget shows the next few things in your day. To draw it, Keystone saves a short summary (the titles and times of those items) in the app's private storage. Those titles appear on your home screen, so anyone who can see your unlocked screen can read them. Remove the widget if you would rather they did not.
- **Reminders and notification buttons.** Reminders are scheduled on your device and appear in your notifications with the title of the goal, event or task, which can show on a lock screen depending on your phone's settings. Buttons such as Done and Snooze act on the data on your device.
- **Share into Keystone (Android).** When you share text, a link or an address from another app to Keystone, it is handled on your device and saved as a task, note or pin only if you choose.
- **Weekly review and check-ins.** The weekly review and the check-in after an event are worked out on your device from your own goals, tasks, events and contacts. Their reminders are local notifications.
- **Usage counts and recovery copies.** "Your week in Keystone" counts how often you used the app and stays on your phone. If cloud sync is on, Keystone also keeps a copy of the last synced version, and one copy from before a merge, in the app's private storage, so a sync can be undone by hand if something goes wrong.

## What can leave your device, and why

Each of these is optional.

- **An account.** Signing in creates an account through our sign-in provider, Clerk, which stores your email address. You do not need an account to use the free features.
- **Cloud sync (Pro).** If you turn this on, your app data (events, goals, tasks, notes, contacts including photos and addresses, map pins and most settings) is stored on our server so it can follow you to another device. When two of your devices have both changed something, your phone combines the changes. The server keeps one copy and does not read or merge it. Settings that belong to one device, such as your haptics choice and your Pro status, are not synced. The data is sent over an encrypted connection and kept until you delete your account. It is not end-to-end encrypted, so we and our hosting provider are able to see it. It can be switched off in Settings → Account and sync.
- **Accountability partners.** If you invite someone, or accept an invite, to be accountability partners, Keystone shares with that one person, and only them, your goal titles and progress and how many of today's tasks are done (not the tasks themselves). Your email address is visible to them. Nothing is shared until they accept. It is removed from our server when you remove your last partner, and either of you can end it at any time.
- **Shared calendars (Pro for more than a few).** If you create or join a shared calendar, the events on that calendar are visible to the people on it. Nothing else in your data is shared.
- **Location.** Keystone only uses your device's location when you use a location feature: dropping a pin, using "Use my location", showing your position on the map, or an arrival reminder you set up. It does not track your location in the background beyond what an arrival reminder you set up needs. Your position stays on your device.
- **Map tiles and address search.** The map loads map pictures from OpenStreetMap and, for the satellite and gray styles, from Esri. Searching the map or entering an address sends the search text to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find coordinates. These requests go straight from your device to those services, not through our server, and they can see your IP address and the area you are viewing. We do not control what they do with that traffic. See their own privacy policies.
- **Google Calendar and Contacts (Pro).** If you connect Google in Settings, Keystone asks for read and write access to your Google Calendar and read-only access to your Google Contacts. Contacts are a one-time import into your local data. The calendar is kept in step while the app is open: single (non-repeating) events you create, edit or delete in Keystone are pushed to Google Calendar, and changes made on Google are pulled in. This only happens while Keystone is open. Our server holds a Google-issued token to do this until you tap Disconnect, which also revokes it. Keystone's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements: the data is used only to show and sync your own events and contacts, is never used for advertising, and is never sold or shared with anyone other than you.
- **Your phone's contacts.** If you tap Import from phone, Android asks for permission to read your contacts. Keystone reads them on the device to show you a list and copies only the ones you tick (name, phone number, email and address) into your Keystone data. It never writes to your phone's contacts, and the permission is not used for anything else.
- **Your phone's calendar.** If you tap Import from phone calendar, Android asks for permission to read your calendar. Keystone lists your calendars on the device and copies the events from the ones you choose (title, date and time, location and notes) into your Keystone calendar. It never changes your phone's calendar, and the permission is not used for anything else. Keystone remembers which calendars and date range you picked, on your device only.
- **Canvas calendar (Pro).** If you paste your Canvas Calendar Feed link into Settings, Keystone fetches it directly from your school's Canvas site, from your phone, to show your assignments and class dates on your calendar. The link stays on your device and is never sent to our server or included in backups or sync. The items it brings in become ordinary events in your data, so they are included in a backup or cloud sync if you use those. Disconnecting removes the link and the items it added. Keystone is not made by or affiliated with Instructure or your school.
- **Links you tap.** Links in your notes open in your browser or another app on your phone. A Canvas link tries the Canvas Student app first if it is installed. Those apps and websites have their own privacy practices. Keystone does not send them anything but the link.
- **Payment (Pro).** Payment is handled entirely by Google Play (or the App Store). Keystone never sees your card details. When you buy Pro, our server confirms the purchase with Google and records that Pro was bought, along with the transaction identifier.
- **Feedback.** "Send feedback" opens your email app with a draft. Nothing is sent until you send it.

## Who else sees it

We use a small number of service providers: Clerk for sign-in, Google (Play purchase checks, and Calendar and Contacts if you connect them), OpenStreetMap and Esri for map pictures and address search, and a hosting provider for the optional sync server. Each one sees only what its job needs. Clerk sees your email. Google sees the access you granted. OpenStreetMap and Esri see the map area or address text you request. The sync server sees your data only if cloud sync is on. None of them may use your data for their own purposes. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

## Where your data is kept and for how long

Data on your phone stays there until you delete it. If you use the optional server features, data is stored on servers run by our hosting provider, which may be in a different country from you, until you delete your account or turn the feature off and remove the data. After an account is deleted, copies can remain in the provider's routine backups for a short time before they are overwritten.

## Security

Data sent to our server is encrypted in transit. Backups you create are encrypted with the password you choose, and we cannot recover that password for you. On your phone, your data is protected by Android and by your phone's lock screen. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone locked and your backup password safe.

## Your data, your control

- Delete individual events, goals, contacts or notes at any time in the app.
- Disconnect Google at any time from Settings. This revokes its access on Google's side and deletes the stored token. It does not remove events or contacts you already imported, which is a separate step on your device.
- Clear all local data from Settings → Your data. This resets the app on that device.
- Delete your account from Settings → Account. This permanently removes your account, your Pro purchase record, and everything stored on our server for it (synced data and shared calendars you own). It does not touch data already saved on your device. You can also email us to request deletion if you cannot sign in.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data. Most of that you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, email us and we will respond. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

## Children's privacy

Keystone is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
